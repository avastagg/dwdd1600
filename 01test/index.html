Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and respectful use of technology and digital tools. It involves knowing how to interact online in positive ways while protecting yourself and others from harm

What is Digital Law

Digital law is the set of rules and legal responsibilities that govern human behavior, technology use, and electronic actions in the digital environment.

What is AUP?

In cybersecurity, an AUP (Acceptable Use Policy) is a formal set of rules written by an organization.

What is Plagiarism

Plagiarism is the act of using another person's words, ideas, or work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

Accidental plagiarism is an actual thing.

What is Academic Dishonesty

Academic dishonesty is any deceptive action that breaks school or research rules. It gives a person an unfair advantage. It happens when a student or researcher tries to get credit for work they did not truly do.

What are the Rules for AI Generated Content?

AI content rules focus on transparency, human accountability, and the prohibition of deceptive synthetic media. Major platforms, legal frameworks (such as the EU AI Act and regional advertising laws), and organizational policies mandate that AI-generated text, images, and deepfakes must be clearly labeled and scrutinized by humans before publication
