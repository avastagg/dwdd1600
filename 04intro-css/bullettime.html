Bullet Time

The Most Iconic Movie Scene to Date.

What Made it Work?

Camera Setup

A rig of 120 (!) Canon EOS and motion picture cameras were placed in a circular track around the actors. The track was built much like a mild roller coaster in that it was not one fluid circle, but many small hills.

Sequential Timing

The cameras fired one after another with slight delays. Think of how stop motion works!

Digital Compositing

The pictures were flawlessly edited together in post so the action looked like a single and fluid shot.

Practicality

Actors were supported by wires in front of green screens to hold extreme poses and prevent fatigue. This allowed for many takes.

Pop Culture Impact

Bullet Time was completely revolutionary. It propelled action movies away from choppy practical stunts into a new era of digital compositing. It inspired many parodies (even showing up in Shrek) as everyone wanted to be a part of this new way to show action in cinema.
